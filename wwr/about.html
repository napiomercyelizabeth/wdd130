History

Founded in 2010, our rafting company started with just two rafts and a deep passion for adventure. Over the past decade, we have safely guided tens of thousands of adventurers down thrilling rapids, building a strong reputation for safety, fun, and expert local guides.

Today, we operate across multiple river systems, offering memorable experiences for both beginners and experienced thrill-seekers. We remain committed to eco-friendly practices, safety excellence, and supporting our local river communities.
